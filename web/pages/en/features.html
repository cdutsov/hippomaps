A map you can ask in plain words

hippomaps is a free map for the web and Android, made with OpenStreetMap data. Type what you are looking for as you would say it, and the map shows each place with its photo, opening hours and the way there.

Open the map

  • Search in plain words“Peaks over 4000 m in Valais”, “bakeries open on Sunday in Zurich”: sentences in 16 languages, read into a query of the map data.
  • Place pagesPhoto, opening hours with the next change, website, phone, wheelchair access, the menu of a restaurant.
  • DirectionsBy car, bike and on foot, with stops, other routes, live traffic in Switzerland and a height profile.
  • Public transportTrains, trams, buses, boats and cable cars across Europe, with platforms, walks to the stops and delays.
  • Spoken navigationTurn by turn in 18 languages, with lanes, speed limits and the map of the route saved for dead spots.
  • Measure and GPS filesDistances, areas and height profiles; routes, lines and places as GPX, KML or GeoJSON.
  • The mapDay and night styles, 3D terrain and buildings, relief shading, transit lines, traffic and the compass of the phone.

Free, without an account

hippomaps needs no account and shows no ads. Settings, starred places and recent searches stay in your browser. The source code is free software under the GPL, on GitLab and GitHub. Maps, search and heights come from overpass.ch, which hosts the services in Switzerland.

On a phone

hippomaps.ch works in the browser of any phone. The app for Android adds navigation with the screen locked, the compass of the phone, and opens links to places from other map apps. It is on the releases page of the project.

New to hippomaps? The guides go through common tasks step by step.
