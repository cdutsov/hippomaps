Guides

Common tasks in hippomaps, step by step. The links in a guide open the map at its example.

  • Searches to tryExample searches, from cafés by a river to peaks over 4000 m. Each opens the map with its results.
  • Plan a bike route with its height profileStart, destination and stops, the climbs in the height profile, and the route as a GPX file.
  • Find a train connectionConnections now or at a set time, with platforms, changes and delays.
  • Put a route on a GPS deviceA route, a measured line or search results as GPX for Garmin, Wahoo, Komoot and other apps; open a GPX file on the map.
  • Navigate without a connectionWhat hippomaps saves on the phone at Start, and how much space it takes.
  • Share a place, a search or a routeLinks that open hippomaps with the same place, search or route.
