Guides

Plan a bike route with its height profile

From Zurich main station to the Uetliberg as the example: 10 km and about 500 m of climb.

  • Open this route
  1. Tap the blue arrow beside the search box. Directions open with your location as the start.
  2. Type the destination, such as “Uetliberg”, and pick it from the list. You can also open a place and tap Directions, or right-click the map (long press on a phone) and choose Directions to here.
  3. To start somewhere else, tap the start and type another place.
  4. Choose Bike. The route appears with its time, distance and the height profile.
  5. To pass a place on the way, add it as a stop: up to 8.

Read the height profile

The profile shows the heights along the route, with the total climb and descent. Move the mouse or a finger along it: a dot on the map shows where that point is. A steep part is easy to find this way before you ride it.

Other routes

When there are other good routes, they appear paler on the map with how much longer they take. Tap one to see its profile.

Water, food and bike shops on the way

Under the route, Along the route lists drinking water, food, cafés, bike shops and more near the route, with how far each is.

Take it along

  • Tap GPX to save the route as a track with its heights, for a bike computer or a watch (more on GPX).
  • Tap Start for spoken navigation. The map along the route is saved on the phone at Start.
  • Share the route as a link (how).
