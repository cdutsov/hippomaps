Guides

Find a train connection

From Zurich main station to Lugano as the example. The same steps work for a tram across town or a bus in the mountains.

  • Open this connection
  1. Tap the blue arrow beside the search box and choose the destination.
  2. Choose Transit as the way to travel.
  3. Leave now is set. To travel later, tap it and choose Depart at or Arrive by, with a date and time.
  4. The connections appear with their times and changes. Tap one to see its legs.
  5. Earlier and Later show more connections.

Read a connection

  • Each leg names its line, the direction, the stop where you get on and off, and the platform or stand.
  • The number of stops tells you when to get off.
  • The walks to the first stop and from the last follow the streets, with their time.
  • The lines are drawn on the map in their colours.

Delays and disruptions

Where an operator publishes live data, the times include delays and a cancelled leg is marked. In Switzerland, messages about disruptions appear with the leg they concern.

Where it works

hippomaps finds connections in 43 regions of Europe and loads their timetables every night. More on the page Public transport.
