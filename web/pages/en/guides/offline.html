Guides

Navigate without a connection

Mobile data often drops in valleys and tunnels. hippomaps saves the map along the route before you set off.

  1. Plan the route while you have a connection.
  2. Tap Start. hippomaps saves the map along the route and the spoken directions on your device.
  3. Set off. When the connection drops, the page says so, and navigation continues along the route with the saved map.

How much space

About 15 MB for 25 km by car. The map is saved for about 350 km of a route at most. hippomaps deletes it 3 days after your last navigation.

The map is saved only while Data Saver is off in the browser.

What needs the connection

  • a new route, also when you leave the planned one
  • searches and place pages
  • the map away from the route

Turning it off

On a plan with little data, turn off Autosave route for offline navigation in the menu. Navigation then loads the map as it goes.

On Android

In the app for Android, navigation also goes on with the screen locked or another app in front. In a browser, keep the page open; hippomaps keeps the screen on while it navigates.
