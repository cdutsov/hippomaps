When a search comes back empty

  • Read the line above the list: it says how the sentence was read. If a word was taken for a place, write the place another way, such as its official name.
  • Try a reading or a place from the buttons under that line.
  • Use a more common word for the kind of place: “cafés” rather than “coffee bars”.
  • Zoom out or move the map. A search that names only a kind of place looks in the view.
