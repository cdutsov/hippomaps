Guides

Share a place, a search or a route

Each Share button makes a link. On a phone it opens the share menu; on a computer it copies the link.

A place

Open the place and tap Share under its photo. A point on the map is shared the same way from What is here. The link opens the map at the place with its page.

A search

The address in the browser holds the search, such as hippomaps.ch/?q=cafés in Zurich by the Limmat. Copy it, or tap Share beside the place of the search. With a result open, the link opens that result.

A route

Tap Share under the route. The link holds the start, the stops, the destination and the way to travel. When the start is your location, the route opens from the location of the person who opens the link.

A view of the map

The address of the page also holds the view, after #map=: the zoom, the place and, in 3D, the angle. Copy it to share what you see, such as the Matterhorn in 3D.

In the app

On a phone with the app for Android, links to hippomaps.ch open in the app. Links from Google Maps, Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap and geo: links can be opened with hippomaps too.
