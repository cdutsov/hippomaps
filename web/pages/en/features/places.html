Features

Place pages

Tap a place on the map or in a list of results. Its page shows what you need before you go.

  • museums in Zurich
  • restaurants with outdoor seating in Lucerne

On the page of a place

  • a photo or logo from Wikimedia Commons; without one, a photo taken there, the picture the website of the place shows when it is shared, or a street picture from Panoramax
  • the opening hours, with whether it is open now and when that changes (“Open · closes 20:30”)
  • the address, website, phone and e-mail
  • wheelchair access, Wikipedia, and every tag of the place in OpenStreetMap
  • for a restaurant, café or bar, a link to its menu on its website

When OpenStreetMap has no opening hours for a place, hippomaps looks for them on its website. The server of hippomaps reads the website, so the business does not see your address.

Buttons

Under the photo: directions to the place, Save to keep it in your list, a link to share, its website, a call, and the place on OpenStreetMap.

What is here

A tap on the map, where there is no place, shows the address of the point and the named places around it. On a computer a right click, on a phone a long press, opens a menu with directions from or to the point, Measure distance, and its coordinates.

Popups

A place of the map that is tapped, or under the mouse on a computer, shows its name, kind and, when known, its opening hours and address, without opening its page.

A mistake on the map?

The data is from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can correct. Feedback in the menu of hippomaps has a link that opens a note on OpenStreetMap at the place you are looking at.
