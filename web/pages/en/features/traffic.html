Features

Live traffic

In Switzerland, the Netherlands and France, hippomaps reads what the road authorities report about the roads every few minutes. The map shows it, and routes by car count it.

  • Open the map at Zurich, then turn on Traffic in the menu
  • By car from Zurich to Bern

On the map

Traffic in the menu draws closures, queues, roadworks, accidents, obstacles, weather warnings, events and slow traffic on the roads. Tap one to read its message, with the road, the length of the queue, the delay, and the source with the time of its report.

In routes by car

The time of a route by car counts the speeds measured on the roads and avoids closed roads. Other routes offered beside the fastest are timed the same way.

The reachable area by car counts the traffic too.

Where it comes from

CountrySourceSpeeds
SwitzerlandFederal Roads Office (FEDRO), Traffic Data Platformevery minute, from counters on the motorways
NetherlandsNDW (Nationaal Dataportaal Wegverkeer)every minute, from about 14,000 measuring points
FranceBison Futéevery 6 minutes, on the national roads without tolls

Signs on the route

A route by car also marks stop signs and speed bumps. Speed cameras are marked only in countries where a navigation app may show them, so not in Switzerland, Germany or France. A warning above Start tells when the route takes a ferry or a car train.

In other countries routes use the usual speeds of each road, without live traffic.
