Features

Search the map in plain words

Write what you want to find and where, as a sentence. hippomaps reads it into a query of OpenStreetMap and draws every place it finds.

  • peaks over 4000 m in Valais
  • bakeries open on Sunday in Zurich
  • charging stations along the A1 in Switzerland

What a sentence can say

A search names a kind of place and where to look. It can add more:

  • a place: a town, a canton or state, a country, a street or a station (“museums in Vienna”)
  • a river, a lake, a road or a border (“cafés by the Limmat”, “charging stations along the A1”, “bridges over the Rhine in Basel”)
  • a distance from something (“hotels near Zurich main station”, “playgrounds near the lake”)
  • a height (“peaks over 4000 m”), opening hours (“open on Sunday”, “open now”) or a feature (“with outdoor seating”, “vegan”, “with wifi”)
  • a way between two places (“hiking trails from Grindelwald to Wengen”)
  • a brand or a name (“Migros in Bern”)

A search that names only a kind of place looks in the part of the map you see.

The answer

The line above the list says how the sentence was read, such as “Cafe, by Limmat (within 150 m) in Zurich”. When a word has more than one meaning, other readings appear as buttons under it, and places of the same name as well. The river, road or border of the search is drawn on the map.

Each result shows its kind, address, a photo or logo, and whether it is open now. Once you zoom or move the map, the list shows the results in view. A tap opens the page of the place.

The results can be downloaded as CSV for a spreadsheet, GeoJSON for a GIS, or GPX waypoints for a GPS device (how).

Languages

The search reads sentences in English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Swedish, Greek, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian and Bulgarian. Place names are understood in their own language and in yours: “Genf”, “Genève” and “Geneva” are the same city.

The search is overpass-search. It reads the sentence with word lists and templates, and the same sentence always gives the same query.
