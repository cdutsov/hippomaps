Features

Public transport in Europe

Choose the train as the way to travel in directions, and hippomaps finds connections by train, tram, metro, bus, boat and cable car, with the walks to and from the stops.

  • From Zurich main station to Lugano

A connection

  • leave now, or set a time to leave or to arrive
  • each leg with its line, the direction, the stop where it starts and ends, the platform, and the number of stops
  • the walks to the first stop and from the last, along the streets
  • the line drawn on the map in its colour
  • later and earlier connections

Delays

Where an operator publishes them, the times include delays, and messages about disruptions appear with the connection. In Switzerland this covers the trains, trams and buses of opentransportdata.swiss.

Where

The timetables cover 43 regions of Europe: the timetables that operators and authorities publish, collected by Transitous. They are loaded again every night. The licences page lists the sources.

Transit lines (metro, light rail and tram) can also be shown on the map in their colours: Transit in the menu.
