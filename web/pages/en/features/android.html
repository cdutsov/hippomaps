Features

hippomaps for Android

The app for Android opens the same map as hippomaps.ch and adds what a browser tab cannot do, such as spoken navigation with the screen locked.

Download the APK

What the app adds

  • navigation goes on with the screen locked or another app in front, with the next turn in a notification
  • the compass of the phone: a cone shows where the phone points, and the map can turn with it
  • location from the GPS of the phone, without Google Play services
  • links to hippomaps.ch from other apps open in the app
  • the back button closes the menu, a place or directions before it leaves the app
  • a screen of its own when there is no connection, which loads the map again once the phone is online

Install

Download the APK of the newest version from the releases page and open it on the phone. Android asks once to allow installs from the browser. The app runs on Android 8 and newer.

Privacy

The app asks for the location when you first use it and for notifications at the first navigation. It does not ask for background location: navigation runs as a service started while the app is in front. hippomaps works without an account and without advertising. The source code is free software under the GPL, on GitLab and GitHub.

In the browser

Everything else works the same in the browser of any phone: open hippomaps.ch. To save or open GPX, KML and GeoJSON files, use the browser, since the app does not handle files yet. The guide Navigate without a connection explains what is kept on the phone for tunnels and valleys.
