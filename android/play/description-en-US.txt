HippoMaps is the Android app of hippomaps.ch, a map with search and navigation built on OpenStreetMap data.

• Search in plain words ("cafés in Zurich old town", "campsites by Lake Geneva") with suggestions while you type
• Place pages with a picture from Wikimedia Commons, opening hours, website, phone, address and wheelchair access
• Directions by car, bike and on foot, with up to 8 stops and places along the route (fuel, charging, rest areas, food)
• Turn-by-turn navigation with spoken instructions; it goes on with the screen locked
• Height profile by bike and on foot
• Live traffic in Switzerland: closures, queues and roadworks
• Metro, light rail and tram lines in their colours
• Nine map styles, a day and night mode, 3D terrain and buildings
• English, German, French and Italian
• Opens geo: links and the map links of other apps

No account, no advertising, no tracking. Settings, saved places and recent searches stay on the phone.

The app shows hippomaps.ch and needs an internet connection. Maps, search and heights come from overpass.ch, routes from Valhalla. The source code is free software (GPL-3.0 or later): https://gitlab.com/cdutsov/hippomaps
